Safety and reporting

LCorals is a free language-exam practice tool. It is not a counselling service or an emergency response service. AI feedback can make mistakes.

If you are worried about your safety or somebody else's

Speak to a trusted adult, teacher, parent or guardian, or seek appropriate professional support. If someone is in immediate danger in Ireland, call 112 or 999.

LCorals cannot identify students, contact a school or parent automatically, or monitor conversations in real time. Do not rely on this website to send an emergency alert.

When the AI interrupts practice

A high-risk safety classification can pause AI feedback. This does not mean you have done anything wrong. Ordinary exam topics such as school bullying, family changes and mental wellbeing should remain suitable for practice. A classifier can sometimes get things wrong.

If it was a false alarm, you can try a different phrasing or report the problem below. Please do not include a full transcript or anyone's private details.

Report a problem with LCorals

Use the existing feedback form to report inappropriate feedback, a mistaken safety block, a technical issue or a privacy concern. Reports are delivered through Formspree; they are not an emergency channel and are not monitored continuously.

Please describe the issue, language and mode. Do not paste a student's complete answer, a phone number, email address or sensitive personal information. Your report is sent to Formspree only if you press Send report.

Privacy and follow-up

Reports are submitted voluntarily. They may be reviewed to fix the reported problem. Without contact details we may not be able to reply, and submission does not guarantee an immediate response. For details about third-party processing, see Privacy & AI.

For teachers: if a real pupil discloses a safeguarding concern, follow your school's child-protection procedures. A report through this website does not replace them.
