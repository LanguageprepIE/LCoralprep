Choose the right level of challenge

The module offers General (Podstawowy) and Discussion (Rozszerzony). These are practice settings: General supports personal answers; Discussion encourages developed opinions. Choose the challenge that helps you improve rather than treating these labels as official exam levels.

Rozmowa: answer with a clear structure

Select a topic in Rozmowa (Interview), listen to the question and answer in Polish. Give your main point first, then a reason and an example. Select Proszę sprawdzić to review feedback and corrections. For discussion practice, add another perspective where it is relevant.

Study Mode: prepare useful language

Choose Study Mode and a topic to explore vocabulary, grammar and speaking prompts. Focus on one useful structure or connector, then return to Exam Mode and use it in an answer of your own. Avoid trying to fit every suggestion into one response.

Mock: practise five conversation questions

Select Start Mock Exam (5 Questions). Answer each prompt, request feedback and continue to the next question. The module currently provides conversation practice; it has no separate portfolio tab or combined full-exam route. Use the topics to rehearse discussing your interests and experiences, and use your teacher’s materials for any additional preparation.

Example: support an opinion

Moim zdaniem sport jest ważny, ponieważ pomaga nam zachować zdrowie. Na przykład regularnie gram w piłkę z przyjaciółmi. This states an opinion, gives a reason and adds an example. Personalise it and check case endings as well as verb forms.

Speak, dictate and listen

Add the Polish keyboard in your device settings. Tap the answer box and use the keyboard microphone to dictate, or type your answer. Set dictation to the language you are speaking and check that the transcript matches your words before submitting. If audio is silent, check the device volume and whether your browser has a suitable language voice. Try another browser or device if needed. The “listen to my answer” control reads the submitted text using a synthetic voice; it does not replay a recording of you.

Use feedback to improve your next attempt

The AI reviews your submitted text. It cannot reliably assess your pronunciation, pace or live interaction from a transcript. Treat scores as practice estimates. Check suggestions that seem doubtful with your teacher. Choose one useful correction and one phrase, then answer again aloud in your own words.

A 15-minute practice routine

  1. 3 minutes: choose one topic and prepare a few useful words or a structure in Study Mode.
  2. 5 minutes: answer aloud, submit your response and read the feedback.
  3. 4 minutes: repeat the answer without reading, then practise one more question or task.
  4. 3 minutes: note one improvement and one target for next time.
Use a separate session for a mock. Try the same topic on another day to see whether you can answer more independently.

Before your next session

  • I can understand the question and answer it directly.
  • I can add a relevant reason or example.
  • I have practised one correction aloud.
  • I know which task or topic to practise next.

Keep this guide handy

Use your browser’s Print option to save a PDF or print the guide. For the controls at a glance, use the Instructions panel on the practice page. For exam requirements and prescribed materials, follow your teacher’s current guidance.
