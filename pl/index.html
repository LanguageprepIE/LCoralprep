📖 Polish practice guide
Instrukcje:
  • ⌨️ Important: Add the Polish Keyboard to your device settings. Use the 🎤 microphone on the Polish keyboard for accurate voice-to-text.
  • 🗣️ Conversation: Select a topic. Practice your oral fluency, discuss personal interests, and prepare for the examiner's questions.
  • 📚 Study Mode: Review key vocabulary and specific Polish grammar with AI help.
  • 📖 Oral Topics: Practice discussing various themes and expressing your opinion clearly, as required in the Polish exam.

Mock exam setup

Conversation and opinion → portfolio discussion → learning reflection. No hints or corrections during the mock.

Use an item from your own language portfolio: a project, text, recording, cultural activity or learning reflection. Discussing it is the task; the portfolio itself is not graded here. General and Discussion are practice labels, not official examination levels.

A shortened, text-based practice mock with optional answer-dependent follow-ups. Final score is a practice estimate, not an official oral mark. Check dictation before submitting.
